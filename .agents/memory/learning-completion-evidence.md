---
name: Learning completion evidence
description: Evidence required before awarding points for completed learning activity
---

Client-synced completion flags for PDFs, notes, or videos do not prove that a student actually completed the activity.

**Why:** awarding points from client-controlled booleans lets users claim rewards without doing the work and undermines leaderboard fairness.

**How to apply:** require a server-validated completion event or server-derived threshold before awarding points; do not treat a synced completion flag alone as evidence.
