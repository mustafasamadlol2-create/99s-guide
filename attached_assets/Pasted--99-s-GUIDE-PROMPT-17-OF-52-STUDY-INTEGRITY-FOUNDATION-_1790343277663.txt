# 99’s GUIDE — PROMPT 17 OF 52

## STUDY INTEGRITY FOUNDATION

## DETERMINISTIC TRUST + EVIDENCE + ANTI-ABUSE CONTRACTS

### NO POINTS — NO PUNISHMENT — NO REVIEW UI — NO AI

Prompts 1–16 have been completed.

Prompt 17 starts the Study Integrity subsystem.

The frozen roadmap remains:

```text
Prompt 17 — Integrity foundation
Prompt 18 — Integrity signals + review
Prompt 19 — Points Ledger schema / compatibility bridge
Prompt 20 — Deterministic Points award
```

Prompt 17 must establish the deterministic integrity primitives that future Points, Recall, Group Focus, MCQ, Flashcards, Focus, and analytics can safely consume.

Do NOT implement Prompt 18 signal persistence/review workflow yet.

Do NOT implement Points.

---

# 1. PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Build one reusable Study Integrity foundation that answers deterministic questions such as:

```text
What happened?
Who claims it happened?
What evidence supports it?
Was the request replayed?
Is the action structurally valid?
Is the timing plausible?
Is this source permitted to make this claim?
Can downstream systems trust this action for future rewards?
```

without yet deciding:

```text
punishment
ban
Points deduction
Mastery penalty
review outcome
fraud verdict
```

