# 1. PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Build a deterministic, server-authoritative Recall candidate engine that:

1. identifies lectures the student has genuinely studied;
2. finds eligible MCQ/Flashcard items inside those lectures;
3. derives bounded weakness evidence;
4. derives bounded forgetting-urgency evidence;
5. derives soft recency preference;
6. scores eligible candidates deterministically;
7. chooses exactly one best candidate;
8. can issue that candidate through Prompt 28’s canonical issuance service;
9. remains private;
10. creates no Points/Mastery/Retention score.
