# 77. CACHE FRESHNESS

Mastery/Retention projections are event-driven, but Retention also changes from time alone.

Therefore D1 Retention rows can become time-stale even without a new projection event.

This is critical.

---

# 78. D1 MUST NOT CALCULATE RETENTION DECAY

Worker cannot advance Retention state itself.

So when:

```text

```

the cached Retention row is stale.
