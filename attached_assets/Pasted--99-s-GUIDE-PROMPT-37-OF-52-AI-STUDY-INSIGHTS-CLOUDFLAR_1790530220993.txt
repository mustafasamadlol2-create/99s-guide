# 1. PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Implement a private AI Study Insights service that:

1. obtains the authenticated student's deterministic `StudyAnalyzerDto`;
2. converts only supported deterministic Analyzer facts into a bounded AI grounding payload;
3. calls **Cloudflare Workers AI only**;
4. asks AI to explain those facts in useful student-friendly language;
5. strictly prevents AI from inventing study metrics;
6. strictly prevents AI from changing Mastery/Retention/Points/Calendar/etc.;
7. validates and sanitizes AI structured output;
8. supports Arabic and English;
9. caches valid private AI insight output;
10. invalidates cache semantically when Analyzer facts change;
11. fails safely when Workers AI is unavailable/quota-limited;
12. preserves a deterministic non-AI Study Analyzer as the authoritative fallback.

This prompt implements an **explanation layer**, not a decision engine.
